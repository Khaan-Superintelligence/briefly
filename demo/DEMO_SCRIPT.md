# Briefly demo script (81 seconds)

Video: `demo/briefly-demo.mp4`. Eight glasses screenshots from the end-to-end run, with captions. No voiceover. Play it as is, or narrate along with the lines below.

## 0:00 Title

"Briefly. Your workday, on your glasses."

## 0:04 Brief

The first thing you see. Next task, mail status, live crypto prices, top headlines. One screen, one glance. The prices are live from CoinGecko, the headlines from Hacker News.

## 0:13 Tasks

Tap once. The day's list. Three tasks, all real, stored on the backend.

## 0:22 Toggle

Tap the first task. It is marked done and saved at once. The header drops from 3 open to 2 open. This is state, not a picture of state.

## 0:31 Ask

Scroll to the last entry and tap. Four preset questions. The glasses have no keyboard, so the questions are the interface.

## 0:40 Answer

"Summarize the top news." Gemini reads the live headlines and answers in one sentence. The sentence on screen was generated during the test run, not written for the demo.

## 0:49 Back

Tap. Back to the questions. Every screen is one tap away from where you were.

## 0:58 Draft

"Draft a reply." Gemini writes a short reply to the teammate's message about this demo. The draft is shown, not sent.

## 1:07 Approve

Tap to approve. The reply is logged to the outbox on the backend. Approved, logged, and nothing left the device without a tap.

## 1:16 End card

Tracks: AI Assistants & Work, plus Exchanges & Stocks. Repo link on screen. Tested on the official simulator.
